# CryptoMas Tokenomics (CMAS)

## 1. Token Distribution

| Allocation | Amount | % | Vesting |
|------------|--------|---|---------|
| Play-to-Earn Rewards | 400,000,000 | 40% | Released over 48 months |
| Public Sale (IDO) | 200,000,000 | 20% | 20% TGE, then 6 months linear |
| Team & Advisors | 150,000,000 | 15% | 12 months cliff, 24 months linear |
| Liquidity Pool | 100,000,000 | 10% | 100% at TGE |
| Marketing & Partnerships | 100,000,000 | 10% | 10% TGE, 18 months linear |
| Treasury / Reserves | 50,000,000 | 5% | Unlocked for ecosystem growth |

**Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 CMAS  
**Initial Circulating:** ~250,000,000 CMAS

---

## 2. Burn Mechanics (Deflation)

Every CMAS transaction in the game burns a portion permanently:

| Activity | Fee | Burn % | Treasury % |
|----------|-----|--------|------------|
| Staked Duel (Winner-take-all) | 15% of pot | 5% | 10% |
| Tournament Entry | 20% of entry | 10% | 10% |
| Spectator Betting | 10% of bet | 5% | 5% |
| Hero Skins (Cosmetic) | 100% of price | 50% | 50% |
| Battle Pass | 750 CMAS | 30% | 70% (to rewards) |
| Revive (Continue after death) | 40 CMAS | 100% | 0% |
| Marketplace (Hero NFT trades) | 5% of trade | 2.5% | 2.5% |

**Target:** Burn 30-40% of total supply within 36 months.

---

## 3. Revenue Model

The platform generates revenue from:

1. **Match Fees** (10% to treasury)
2. **Tournament Fees** (10% to treasury)
3. **NFT Marketplace Fees** (2.5% to treasury)
4. **Battle Pass Sales** (70% recycled to rewards, 30% net)

**Treasury Usage:**
- 40% Game development & servers
- 30% Marketing & user acquisition
- 20% Community rewards & airdrops
- 10% Team operations

---

## 4. Staking & Governance (gCMAS)

Players can lock CMAS to receive **gCMAS** (governance tokens):

| Lock Period | CMAS Required | gCMAS Received | Benefits |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| 30 days | 1,000 CMAS | 1 gCMAS | Voting rights |
| 90 days | 1,000 CMAS | 1.2 gCMAS | Voting + fee discount |
| 180 days | 1,000 CMAS | 1.5 gCMAS | Voting + fee discount + VIP queue |

**gCMAS Powers:**
- Vote on new heroes, maps, game modes
- Propose tournament rule changes
- Earn share of treasury dividends (quarterly)

## 5. Player Economics (Example)

**Scenario:** 10,000 daily active players, average 5 duels/day

| Metric | Calculation | Daily Volume |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| Duels played | 10,000 × 5 | 50,000 duels |
| Avg stake | 100 CMAS | 5,000,000 CMAS |
| Match fees (15%) | 5M × 0.15 | 750,000 CMAS |
| Burn (5%) | 750K × 0.33 | 250,000 CMAS |
| Treasury (10%) | 750K × 0.66 | 500,000 CMAS |

**Monthly burn:** ~7,500,000 CMAS  
**Annual burn:** ~90,000,000 CMAS (9% of supply)

---

## 6. Anti-Inflation Safeguards

- **Mint Authority** retained only for Play-to-Earn pool (400M cap)
- **No new minting** after P2E pool depleted
- **Dynamic matchmaking** prevents bot farming
- **Daily claim caps** per player (anti-sybil)
