# Regulatory Compliance Notes

## Jurisdiction: Paraguay

### Current Status (2026)
- Paraguay has minimal crypto-specific regulation
- No specific licensing required for utility tokens
- Corporate tax: 10% (one of lowest globally)
- No capital gains tax on crypto (as of 2026)

### Recommended Actions
1. Register S.A. (Sociedad Anónima) in Paraguay
2. Obtain RUC (tax ID)
3. Open corporate bank account
4. Maintain accounting records in Guaraní

## Skill-Based Gaming vs Gambling

### Why CryptoMas is NOT gambling:
- **100% skill-based** duels (no RNG in outcome)
- **Equal heroes** for free and paying players
- **Matchmaking** pairs similar skill levels
- **Free heroes** available to all players

### Risk Areas
- Some jurisdictions may classify staking as wagering
- Recommended geoblocks: USA, Belgium, Netherlands (until legal review)
- Age verification: 18+ required
